
The redesign and the single-change variants were throwaway patches of `memory.py` and are not kept;
this table is their record. None was adopted: no single change moves more than one row net, and the
redesign loses five (splitting short Chinese runs turns fillers such as `上了` and `了吗` into words, and
ordering partial matches by coverage lets one rare word outrank topics that match the rest).
