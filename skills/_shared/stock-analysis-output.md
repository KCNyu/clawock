# Interactive stock analysis output

For HK/US interactive Modes 2–4. Scheduled Modes 6/7 follow their own payload and
postflight schema; this file adds no scheduled reads.

- Technical read: use the pipeline’s actual indicators and dated price history.
  State trend, support/resistance, and what invalidates the view. Missing history
  means the level is unknown; a familiar RSI threshold alone is no trade authorization.
- Fundamental read: explain business quality, financial trends, cash conversion,
  dilution/debt, valuation versus comparable peers, and the strongest contrary case.
  Cite source, reporting period, currency and units. Distinguish trailing from forward
  estimates and reported from adjusted earnings; disclose missing inputs and assumptions.
  Calculate ratios from compatible periods; do not fill gaps with typical sector values.
- Full report: lead with the judgment, then evidence for and against, valuation
  assumptions/range, technical invalidation, material risks, dated catalysts and next
  review trigger. Use only sections needed for the question. A comparison uses the
  same dates, units and assumptions for both names and explains the preferred tradeoff.
- Prices come from the workspace quote pipeline; filings and announcements establish
  business facts. Mark unavailable sources explicitly. Targets and probabilities are
  estimates with assumptions, never observed facts. Any proposed position change still
  follows the investment SOP, existing authorization and market execution constraints.

Indicator definitions, common ratio formulas and report-writing basics need no
additional reference load. Use tools for current facts and calculations.
